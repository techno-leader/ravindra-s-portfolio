Case Study 01 — Conversion Design · CRO

Same copy. Same product. Different design.

At The Ayurveda Experience (TAE) we run performance marketing driven ads and long form sales pages. This is an example of landing page CRO redesign I did for ViewRyt an eye supplement by TAE.

Team1 Designer (me) · 1 Copywriter
BrandThe Ayurveda Experience — 2.1M customers, 25+ countries
ProductViewRyt — eye supplement
00 — The Product

Strong product, right audience.

ViewRyt is an eye supplement formulated by TAE scientists, inspired by the industry-standard AREDS 2 formula for macular health.

TAE has strong targeting for women 50+ in the US as a natural skincare brand, and the product was deemed a strong fit.

01 — The Problem

The old page (NLP7) converted at 1.44% — and lost money on every ad dollar.

ViewRyt carries the full AREDS 2 formula — the NIH clinical standard shown to reduce advanced AMD risk by 25%. A genuinely strong product. But the page presenting it returned $0.32 for every $1.00 of ad spend.

The product wasn't the problem. The page's decision architecture was. Diagnosis after auditing NLP7
Fault 01Poor targeting

The imagery represented the target audience but never connected emotionally — it read as synthetic stock, not lived experience.

Fault 02Cognitive overload

Dense information hit visitors before the first CTA — thinking hard before caring at all.

Fault 03Improper hierarchy

A single heading size throughout, body type larger than the subhead, and all attention pulled to one photo.

Fault 04Style inconsistency

Comic illustration sat beside photography, shadows appeared at random, and the color palette had no through-line.

Evidence — NLP7, as it shipped
Nothing in the type scale separates a claim from a caption — the body copy even reads larger than the subhead above it.
The heatmap puts 55% of attention on the photo and only 5% on the subhead carrying the promise.
Copy itself is very dense and technical, which needed simpler illustrations.
Stock models and synthetic imagery represented the audience age group, but didn't feel emotionally connecting.
Unnecessary drop shadows on elements, comic illustrations and a broken colour palette all needed to be fixed.

02 — Principles

Four rules for the redesign, copy untouched.

Keeping the copy identical made the test honest: whatever moved, design moved it. NLP8 was built on four principles.

Principle 01Trust first

Establish authority before making a single claim.

Principle 02Earn attention before explaining

Hook emotionally, then educate — never the reverse.

Principle 03Decision architecture

Design the moment of choice, not just the layout around it.

Principle 04Progressive disclosure

Detail arrives exactly when the visitor is ready for it.

03 — Improvements

Four principles that did the heavy lifting.

Improvements — NLP8, as it shipped
Johns Hopkins, the NIH and Yale sit above the first claim, so authority is established before anything is asserted.
The AREDS 2 result is drawn as a chart instead of stated as a number, which makes the evidence legible at a glance.
Normal, early and advanced vision are shown against something worth keeping, so the stake is felt before it is explained.
A clean anatomical illustration carries the clinical terms, which keeps the section skimmable.
Colour leads the eye from the herb through to the hero ingredient, so the comparison resolves into one answer.
Features, trust and the guarantee are framed in a single screen at the point of decision.
A real type scale now separates the claim, the subhead and the supporting line.
The palette was cut back to five deliberate colours, so nothing competes with the call to action.

04 — The Redesign, Annotated

Walk the page. Every section has a job.

This is the shipped NLP8 page, exactly as it runs in production. Keep scrolling — the phone walks through the full page while the notes explain the intent behind each section.

Design intent · section by section
HookThe lead is a story, not a pitch

"5 Silent Signs of Macular Degeneration" opens on the fear of losing independence, then explains why vision degrades after 40. Attention is earned before a single claim is made.

EvidenceThe AREDS-2 study leads, ingredients follow

NIH, 10 years, 4,203 participants — then each nutrient card answers "why it matters." Authority arrives before any brand talk.

RevealThe product is the conclusion

ViewRyt only appears once the argument is complete — entering as the obvious answer, with expert endorsements beside it.

CloseThe CTA is a zone, not a button

Verified reviews, a price-anchored comparison and the 90-day guarantee cluster around the click. The decision moment is designed, not decorated.

06 — Reflection

What I'd push for next time.

The honest caveat: this was a sequential comparison, not a simultaneous A/B split. Next time I'd push for proper A/B infrastructure from day one, plus scroll-depth tracking and heatmaps to see decisions, not just outcomes.

Tests already queued: a video testimonial above the fold, a shorter mobile-first variant, and an earlier price anchor.
